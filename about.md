# Policy Lens - About

## What is Policy Lens?

Policy Lens is a comprehensive digital platform designed to bridge the gap between government dental schemes and the citizens who need them most. We believe that quality dental healthcare should be accessible to everyone, regardless of their economic background or geographic location.

## Why Policy Lens Exists?

### The Problem We Solve

Every year, millions of citizens miss out on valuable dental healthcare benefits simply because they don't know these schemes exist. The current system suffers from:

- **Information Fragmentation**: Dental schemes are scattered across multiple government websites and departments
- **Complex Eligibility**: Citizens struggle to understand if they qualify for specific schemes
- **Application Barriers**: The process to apply for dental benefits is often confusing and time-consuming
- **Awareness Gaps**: Many eligible citizens never discover the schemes designed to help them

### Our Solution

Policy Lens brings all dental healthcare schemes into one intelligent, easy-to-use platform that:

- **Centralizes Information**: All dental schemes in one place, searchable and accessible
- **Simplifies Eligibility**: Smart algorithms help citizens quickly determine their eligibility
- **Streamlines Applications**: Guided application processes with document management
- **Ensures Awareness**: Proactive notifications about relevant schemes based on user profiles
